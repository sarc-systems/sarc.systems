Alan R. Pearlman (1925–2019) was an American electrical engineer who worked on amplifier design for NASA's Gemini and Apollo programs before founding Tonus Inc. in 1969 — renamed ARP Instruments after his own childhood nickname — going on to design the ARP 2500 and, with engineer Dennis Colin, the ARP 2600.
