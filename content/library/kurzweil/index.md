Founded in 1982 by [Ray Kurzweil](/library/ray-kurzweil/), Stevie Wonder, and software developer Bruce Cichowlas, Kurzweil Music Systems grew directly out of Kurzweil's earlier work on the Kurzweil Reading Machine — [Stevie Wonder](/library/stevie-wonder/), an early user of the reading machine, pushed Kurzweil to apply its signal-processing research to music. The company's first product, the K250, launched at NAMM in 1984 at around $10,000 and was the first instrument to convincingly reproduce acoustic grand piano and other orchestral sounds through [digital sampling](/library/digital-sampling/). Kurzweil sold the company to the Korean piano manufacturer Young Chang in 1990; Young Chang was acquired by Hyundai Development Company in 2006, and the Kurzweil brand continues today making keyboards and digital pianos.
