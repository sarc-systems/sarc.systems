Founded by Tom Oberheim in Santa Monica in 1969, Oberheim Electronics built the SEM, DMX drum machine, and the programmable Xpander and Matrix-12 synthesizers before its 1985 bankruptcy; Oberheim regained rights to his own name in 2009 and resumed building instruments under the brand.
