[John Fahey](/library/john-fahey/) used an inheritance from his father's 1995 death to found Revenant Records in 1996 with Dean Blackwood, dedicating the label to archival reissues of blues, old-time, and experimental recordings alongside new work by artists including Derek Bailey and Cecil Taylor. Its 2001 Charley Patton box set, *Screamin' and Hollerin' the Blues*, won three Grammy Awards in 2003.
