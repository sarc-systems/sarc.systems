Founded in 1958 with Ford and Rockefeller Foundation funding, the Instituto Torcuato Di Tella grew from a modest initiative into centers spanning scientific and artistic research, theater, and music — becoming the most significant cultural institution in Buenos Aires through the 1960s, and home to Alberto Ginastera's CLAEM.
