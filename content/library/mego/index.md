Founded in Vienna in 1994 by Ramon Bauer, Andi Pieper, and Peter Meininger — its name short for "My Eyes Glaze Over," borrowed from futurist Herman Kahn — Mego released its first record, *Fridge Trax* by General Magic & Pita, the same year. Meininger left in 1995, replaced by Peter Rehberg, whose own *Seven Tons for Free* (1996, as Pita) helped lay the foundation for what became known as glitch. The label folded in 2005 after a financial breakdown; Rehberg relaunched its catalog and mission the following year as Editions Mego.
