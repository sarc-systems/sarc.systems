Founded in Malvern, Pennsylvania, by former MOS Technology/Commodore engineers including Bob Yannes and Al Charpentier, who had designed the Commodore 64's SID sound chip. The team incorporated in 1983 as Peripheral Visions, planning an Atari 2600 keyboard peripheral, and renamed itself Ensoniq after the 1983 video-game crash pushed them toward music synthesis instead — building the affordable [Mirage](/library/ensoniq-mirage/) sampler and [ESQ-1](/library/ensoniq-esq-1/) synthesizer/sequencer.
