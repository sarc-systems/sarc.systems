James Tenney's *Meta-Hodos* (1961) and its later supplement *META Meta-Hodos*
(1975), collected in this Frog Peak edition, adapt Gestalt psychology to the
perception of musical form — how a listener groups a stream of sound into
elements, clangs, and sequences. For a studio concerned with instruments as
systems and with the feedback between structure and perception, Tenney's
vocabulary is a durable analytic tool: it treats musical form as something the
listener *constructs*, not merely something the score dictates.
