Miles Davis (1926–1991) repeatedly redirected the course of jazz, from the cool jazz of *Birth of the Cool* through modal jazz's *Kind of Blue* to the electric fusion of *Bitches Brew* (1970), recorded with an expanded electric ensemble and assembled from studio jams by producer Teo Macero into a foundational work of jazz fusion.
