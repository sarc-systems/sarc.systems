Founded on 1 April 1958 in Room 13 of the BBC's Maida Vale Studios by Desmond Briscoe and Daphne Oram, after the pair had spent 1957 producing electronic sound out-of-hours for BBC drama, the Radiophonic Workshop supplied theme music and sound effects across BBC radio and television for four decades. Oram left within a year to found her own studio; Briscoe ran the Workshop until 1983. Delia Derbyshire, who realized the *Doctor Who* theme there in 1963, was among its best-known composers.
