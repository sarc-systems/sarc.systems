Masaya Nakahara (b. 1970, Tokyo) has recorded as Violent Onsen Geisha since 1988, releasing through Boid and Daisyworld — *Excrete Music* (1991), *Otis* (1993), *Que Sera, Sera* (1995), *The Midnight Gambler* (1996) — before largely moving to a second alias, Hair Stylistics, from the late 1990s on. Outside music Nakahara is a prolific novelist and film critic, and co-starred in Shinji Aoyama's 2005 film *My God, My God, Why Hast Thou Forsaken Me?* alongside Tadanobu Asano. His remix and collaborative credits sit closer to Japan's indie and experimental-pop circles than the Osaka noise scene — including the 1997 session *Reprovisers* alongside [Jim O'Rourke](/library/jim-orourke/) and [Microstoria](/library/microstoria/), plus work with Cornelius — and no documented collaboration or release ties him to Merzbow, C.C.C.C., or the other Japanoise artists in this catalog.
