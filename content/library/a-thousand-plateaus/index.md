Published in France in 1980 as *Mille Plateaux*, the second volume of *Capitalism and Schizophrenia* abandons linear argument for a set of largely independent "plateaus" that can be read in any order — a structural enactment of the book's own central figure, the rhizome, a non-hierarchical network with no single root or trunk, opposed to the tree-like, centered structures Deleuze and [Guattari](/library/felix-guattari/) associate with Western thought. Introduces or elaborates concepts including the body without organs, deterritorialization, the war machine, and smooth versus striated space. English translation by Brian Massumi (University of Minnesota Press, 1987).
