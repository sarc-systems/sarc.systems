As a student programmer at Dartmouth College, Cameron Jones worked with Sydney Alonso on computer-controlled tone generation and control methods that became the Synclavier, co-founding New England Digital with Alonso in 1976 to commercialize it; Jones later continued the Synclavier lineage through Synclavier Digital.
