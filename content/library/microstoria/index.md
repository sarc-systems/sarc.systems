Microstoria was the experimental electronic duo of Markus Popp (Oval) and Jan St. Werner (Mouse on Mars), formed in Düsseldorf in 1994, refining the glitch-derived language both brought from their solo and other-group work into subtler, more spacious ambient electronics.
