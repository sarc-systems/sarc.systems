Originally released in 1975 on A.R.C. Records, *Brainwave Music* documents David Rosenboom's early biofeedback compositions, in which performers wired with electrodes generated brainwave, temperature, and skin-response data that Rosenboom fed into the electronic systems shaping the music. This 2019 Black Truffle edition expands the original LP with over 40 minutes of contemporaneous material.
