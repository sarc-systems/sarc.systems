Composed at the BBC Radiophonic Workshop for a documentary on the Tuareg, *Blue Veils and Golden Sand* builds its shimmering, heat-haze texture from a single struck metal lampshade, analyzed and re-synthesized through filtered oscillators, alongside Delia Derbyshire's own voice cut up beyond recognition.
