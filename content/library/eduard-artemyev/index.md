Eduard Artemyev (1937–2022) was a Russian composer who became a central figure in Moscow's early electronic-music avant-garde, working on the pioneering ANS synthesizer built by engineer Yevgeny Murzin. He is best known for scoring Andrei Tarkovsky's *Solaris* (1972), *The Mirror* (1975), and *Stalker* (1979), the last built from a blend of natural and railroad noise, processed traditional instruments, and synthesizer drone, across a career spanning over 150 films.
