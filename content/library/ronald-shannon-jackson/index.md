Ronald Shannon Jackson (1940–2013) recorded with [Albert Ayler](/library/albert-ayler/) in 1966–67 — Ayler, he later said, was the first leader who "really opened me up" — before joining [Ornette Coleman](/library/ornette-coleman/)'s electric Prime Time ensemble from 1975 to 1976, recording *Dancing in Your Head* and *Body Meta*. In 1978 he played on four [Cecil Taylor](/library/cecil-taylor/) albums, including *Cecil Taylor Unit* and *3 Phasis*, before co-founding the free-jazz/noise supergroup [Last Exit](/library/last-exit/) with Sonny Sharrock, Peter Brötzmann, and Bill Laswell in 1986, active until Sharrock's death in 1994.
