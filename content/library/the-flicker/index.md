Made on a $300 budget, Tony Conrad's *The Flicker* is built, past its opening credits, entirely from solid black and white frames alternating at varying rates — no camera image at all — producing stroboscopic effects Conrad conceived out of his own study of experimental psychology and flicker-induced hallucination, alongside Brion Gysin's contemporary Dream Machine. Thirty minutes long, it became a landmark of American structural filmmaking.
