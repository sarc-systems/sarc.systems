Christian Fennesz (b. 1962, Neusiedl am See, Austria) moved from guitar in the Austrian rock band Maische into laptop composition in the mid-1990s, signing to Vienna's [Mego](/library/mego/) label and processing guitar through Max/MSP and custom effects to blend melody with glitch and granular treatment. His 2001 album *Endless Summer* became a defining work of the label's catalog. From 1997 he was a member of Fenn O'Berg, an improvised computer-music trio with [Peter Rehberg](/library/peter-rehberg/) and [Jim O'Rourke](/library/jim-orourke/) that toured through 1998–99 with a stage setup of laptops alone, releasing on Mego/Editions Mego through 2010's *In Stereo*.
