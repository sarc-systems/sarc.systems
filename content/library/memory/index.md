The capacity of a system to retain some trace of its past states and let that trace influence future behavior, rather than responding to the present input alone. In cybernetics, memory is what distinguishes a system that can learn or adapt from one that merely reacts; in computation, it is the storage a [state machine](/library/state-machine/) or [Turing machine](/library/turing-machine/) consults and updates at each step; in an instrument, it is what lets recorded material — as in [digital sampling](/library/digital-sampling/) — be stored, played back, and manipulated.
