A 1987 collaborative LP (Silent Records, edition of 500; reissued on CD by Matchless Recordings in 1995) pairing AMM percussionist Eddie Prévost with David Jackman's Organum, one side each — Prévost's bowed and resonant percussion answered by Organum's dense, ritualistic drone.
