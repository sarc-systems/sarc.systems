Born in Queens, New York, in 1948, Ray Kurzweil founded Kurzweil Computer Products in 1974, developing the first omni-font optical character recognition software, the first CCD flatbed scanner, and the first text-to-speech synthesizer, combined into the Kurzweil Reading Machine for the blind, unveiled in 1976. [Stevie Wonder](/library/stevie-wonder/) heard of the machine on television and became an early customer; the two became friends, and in 1982 Wonder's frustration with the limits of studio keyboards led Kurzweil to apply the reading machine's underlying signal-processing work to music synthesis, founding [Kurzweil Music Systems](/library/kurzweil/) with Wonder that same year. Kurzweil later became widely known as a futurist and AI theorist, notably for *The Singularity Is Near* (2005), and holds a Director of Engineering role at Google.
