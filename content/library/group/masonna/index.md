Born Takushi Yamazaki in Miyazu, Kyoto, in 1966, Maso Yamazaki started Masonna in Osaka in 1987, its name a pun on Madonna combining the Japanese words for "masochist" and "woman." Releasing chiefly through Alchemy Records — where Yamazaki also managed the Osaka noise store Alchemy Music until its closure — and later Relapse, Alien8, and Blast First, Masonna became known for wild, destructive live sets, sometimes lasting only seconds, that saw Yamazaki damage his own equipment and occasionally injure himself; the project opened Japanese dates for Sonic Youth, Beck, and Slipknot, and was featured on John Peel's BBC radio program.

Yamazaki formed the noise trio Flying Testicle with [Merzbow](/library/merzbow/)'s Masami Akita and Zev Asher, releasing *Lamerican Sextom* (1992) and *Space Desia* (1993); Akita, Yamazaki, and Zev Asher had first played together, backed by an evolving Osaka lineup, in the freak-rock supergroup Bustmonster (1991–94), which also drew in [C.C.C.C.](/library/cccc/)'s Fumio Kosakai, [Monde Bruits](/library/monde-bruits/)'s Shohei Iwasaki, and [Solmania](/library/solmania/)'s Masahiko Ohno at various points. Yamazaki also recorded with [Aube](/library/aube/)'s Akifumi Nakajima as Kinkakuji and, joined by C.C.C.C.'s Hiroshi Hasegawa, as Gokurakuji.
