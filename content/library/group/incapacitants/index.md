Formed in Osaka in 1981 by Toshiji Mikawa and Fumio Kosakai before relocating to Tokyo, Incapacitants pursue what Kosakai calls "hard noise" — a term borrowed from jazz — built from feedback, vocals, and electronics toward a "pure" noise deliberately stripped of musical intention, released chiefly through Alchemy Records. Kosakai also occasionally contributed to [C.C.C.C.](/library/cccc/), formed in Osaka in 1989, and both Mikawa and Kosakai later became regular members of the noise group Hijokaidan. Day jobs — Mikawa at a bank, Kosakai in a government office — have limited the duo's international touring.
