Dave Smith (1950–2022) founded Sequential Circuits in San Jose in 1974, designing the Prophet-5 — the first fully programmable polyphonic synthesizer — in 1978. He was the driving force behind the MIDI specification in 1981, coining the acronym itself, and later commissioned Dave Rossum to design the 2140 filter for Sequential's instruments. After Sequential Circuits closed in 1987, Smith continued building synthesizers as Dave Smith Instruments from 2002, eventually reclaiming and rebranding back to the Sequential name in 2018.
