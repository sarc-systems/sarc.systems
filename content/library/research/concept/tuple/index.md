A finite, ordered sequence of elements, which may be of the same kind or mixed — a pair, a triple, or any longer fixed-length list where position carries meaning (the first element of a coordinate pair is not interchangeable with the second). A [vector](/library/research/vector/) is a tuple whose elements are numbers meant to be added and scaled together; the same structure, unconstrained to numbers, underlies database records, function arguments, and structured data generally.
