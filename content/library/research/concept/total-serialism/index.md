Total (or integral) serialism extends [serialism](/library/serialism/)'s ordered-series principle beyond pitch to duration, dynamics, register, and articulation. Olivier Messiaen's *Mode de valeurs et d'intensités* (1949) provided an unordered series across all these parameters at once; Pierre Boulez ordered it into rows for *Structures I* (1952), while [Milton Babbitt](/library/milton-babbitt/) worked toward integral serialism independently in the United States from 1947–48. [Karlheinz Stockhausen](/library/karlheinz-stockhausen/) went further still, developing distributive serial processes that avoided recurring rows altogether to eliminate any trace of theme.
