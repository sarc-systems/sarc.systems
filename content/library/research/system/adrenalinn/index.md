The AdrenaLinn is a digital multi-effects unit combining a drum machine, amp modeler, and tempo-synced filter effects in one box, designed by [Roger Linn](/library/roger-linn/) through Roger Linn Design with help from [Dave Smith](/library/dave-smith/), credited with helping conceive MIDI, and [Tom Oberheim](/library/tom-oberheim/), designer of early analog synthesizers.
