Eddie Prévost (b. 1942) is a British percussionist who, alongside Lou Gare, Lawrence Sheaff, and Keith Rowe, co-founded AMM in 1965, developing a percussion practice built from bowed cymbals, resonant drums, and found objects rather than conventional time-keeping. In 1979 he founded Matchless Recordings to document AMM's work and that of related improvisers outside conventional industry structures, and has since written on improvisation in *No Sound Is Innocent* (1995) and *Minute Particulars* (2004).
