Kiyoshi Mizutani met [Masami Akita](/library/research/masami-akita/) in junior high school in Tokyo and played improvised rock with him before the two co-founded [Merzbow](/library/research/merzbow/) in 1979, remaining the project's other constant member — performing on its early recordings and live shows — through the 1980s. He left after Merzbow's 1989 European tour to pursue a solo career, releasing electronic noise built from feedback and instrumentation alongside compositions drawing on field recording, beginning with *Actual Infinity / 2 Variations* and *The Same Thing Makes Always Her Laugh* (both 1990). *Works 1989-1991*, issued on cassette by the Osaka label Povertech Industries, documents the recordings made around his departure from Merzbow.
