François Bayle (b. 1932, Toamasina, Madagascar) studied in the 1950s with Olivier Messiaen, [Pierre Schaeffer](/library/pierre-schaeffer/), and [Karlheinz Stockhausen](/library/karlheinz-stockhausen/) before joining French state broadcasting (ORTF) in 1960. He led the [GRM](/library/grm/) from 1966, and when it was absorbed into the newly formed Institut national de l'audiovisuel in 1975 became its director, a position he held until 1997. Across that tenure he coined the term "acousmatic music" and developed the Acousmonium, a large multi-speaker system for diffusing tape music in performance. In 1991 he established his own Studio Magison for composition and research, continuing to work there after leaving the GRM's directorship.
