Jon Appleton directed Dartmouth College's electronic music program, where his dissatisfaction with existing synthesizers set students Cameron Jones and Sydney Alonso to work in 1976 on what became the Synclavier — Appleton stayed closely involved in defining its user interface and musician-facing ergonomics as the two engineers built it. He went on to compose extensively on the instrument, including *The Sweet Dreams of Miss Pamela Beach*.
