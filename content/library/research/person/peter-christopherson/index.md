Peter "Sleazy" Christopherson (1955–2010) was a partner in the album-cover design group Hipgnosis, designing sleeves including Pink Floyd's *Wish You Were Here* and Peter Gabriel's first three solo albums, before co-founding [Throbbing Gristle](/library/research/throbbing-gristle/) in 1975. After the group's 1981 split he formed Psychic TV with [Genesis P-Orridge](/library/research/genesis-p-orridge/) and Alex Fergusson, playing on its first two albums, then left with John Balance to found [Coil](/library/research/coil/) in 1984. He died in his sleep in Bangkok in November 2010.
