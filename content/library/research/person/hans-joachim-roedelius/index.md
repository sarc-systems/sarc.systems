Hans-Joachim Roedelius (b. 1934) co-founded the Zodiak Free Arts Lab in West Berlin in 1968 with [Conrad Schnitzler](/library/conrad-schnitzler/), the venue where he met Dieter Moebius; the two, with Schnitzler, formed the improvising trio Kluster in 1969, continuing as [Cluster](/library/cluster/) after Schnitzler's 1971 departure. In 1973 Roedelius and Moebius formed [Harmonia](/library/harmonia/) with [Neu!](/library/neu/)'s Michael Rother, and Roedelius began a prolific solo career in 1978 with *Durch die Wüste*, extending over the following decades into the long-running *Selbstportrait* series and more than a hundred further releases.
