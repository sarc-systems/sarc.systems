Published by Prentice-Hall in 1980 and revised and expanded by the British Library Sound Archive in 1992, [Derek Bailey](/library/research/derek-bailey/)'s *Improvisation: Its Nature and Practice in Music* was described on release as the first book to treat the nature of improvisation across all its forms as a single subject — Indian music, flamenco, baroque and organ music, rock, jazz, contemporary composition, and [free improvisation](/library/research/free-improvisation/) — built substantially from Bailey's own conversations with improvisers including John Zorn, Steve Lacy, Max Roach, and Evan Parker.
