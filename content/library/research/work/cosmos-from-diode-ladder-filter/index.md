Released in 2001 on Alchemy Records, *Cosmos From Diode Ladder Filter* marks [Space Machine](/library/space-machine/)'s (Maso Yamazaki's) turn away from the harsh noise of his Masonna project toward non-rhythmic, purely analog electronic soundscapes, made entirely on vintage gear — EMS VCS3, Roland systems, and analog echo chambers — at his Space Machine Systems Studio in Osaka, in a lineage closer to Klaus Schulze's early Kosmische synthesizer work than to noise. Its eleven untitled pieces include collaborations with Makoto Kawabata (Acid Mothers Temple) and Hiroshi Hasegawa of [C.C.C.C.](/library/cccc/). Reissued on vinyl by Urashima in 2022.
