Recorded in September 1994 and released that October on [Pete Namlook](/library/pete-namlook/)'s FAX label, *The Dark Side of the Moog* — dedicated to [Robert Moog](/library/robert-moog/) — paired Namlook's ambient, sample-based production with [Klaus Schulze](/library/klaus-schulze/)'s sequenced analog synthesizer work at a point when Schulze, already deep into a long solo career, was embracing the newer sampling technology Namlook worked in. The pairing proved lasting: what began as a single album grew into an eleven-volume series recorded between 1994 and 2008, one of the longest-running collaborative threads in either artist's catalog.
