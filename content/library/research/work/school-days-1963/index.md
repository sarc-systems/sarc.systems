Recorded live, probably in March 1963, at the Phase Two Coffee House in New York, *School Days* documents [Steve Lacy](/library/steve-lacy/)'s quartet with Roswell Rudd, Henry Grimes, and [Denis Charles](/library/denis-charles/) working through Thelonious Monk repertoire ("Bye-Ya," "Brilliant Corners," "Monk's Dream," "Monk's Mood," "Ba-lue Bolivar Ba-lues-are," "Skippy," "Pannonica") years before Lacy's own Monk-focused recordings became widely known — this quartet was among the first groups anywhere built entirely around Monk's compositions. Released for the first time in 1975 on Emanem.
