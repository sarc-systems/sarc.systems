Released on Columbia in 1973 and recorded at Wally Heider's and Different Fur Trading Co. in San Francisco, *Sextant* is the last and most electronically dense studio album by Herbie Hancock's Mwandishi sextet before it disbanded. [Patrick Gleeson](/library/patrick-gleeson/), hired as a synthesizer technician on the previous album *Crossings* (1972), returned on ARP 2600, threading modular synthesis through Hancock's electric piano, clavinet, and Mellotron across three long, loosely structured pieces ("Rain Dance," "Hidden Shadows," "Hornets"). The album is a high-water mark for synthesizer integration into acoustic jazz performance practice in the early 1970s, predating Hancock's later, more song-based electronic work on *Sunlight* (1978) and *Future Shock* (1983).
