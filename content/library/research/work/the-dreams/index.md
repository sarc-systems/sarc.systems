The first of the BBC Third Programme's *Inventions for Radio* series, *The Dreams* pairs writer Barry Bermange's edited interviews of people recounting their dreams with Delia Derbyshire's electronic sound collage, structured across five movements — running, falling, landscape, underwater, and colour — into an early, pioneering fusion of spoken word and electronic sound design.
