Released in 1985 as one of the first two titles on [Barry Truax](/library/research/barry-truax/)'s own Cambridge Street Records — founded that year as an alternative to commercial distribution for computer and electroacoustic music — *Sequence of Earlier Heaven* gathers a cycle of four pieces composed between 1981 and 1985 and organized around the I Ching: *East Wind* (1981) and *Nightwatch* (1982) pair live recorder and marimba against tape, while *Wave Edge* (1983) and *Solar Ellipse* (1984-85) extend the cycle into purely electronic and computer-generated material developed on Truax's own systems at Simon Fraser University.
