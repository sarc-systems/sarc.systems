Recorded in Paris in 1979 under producer Martin Meissonnier and originally issued on the Japanese label Paddle Wheel, *Session in Paris Vol. 1: Song of Soil* documents a trio session by Japanese free-jazz percussionist [Masahiko Togashi](/library/research/masahiko-togashi/) with trumpeter [Don Cherry](/library/research/don-cherry/) and bassist Charlie Haden, both longtime associates of [Ornette Coleman](/library/research/ornette-coleman/)'s bands. Its six tracks move between spiritual jazz, Eastern-inflected modal playing, and open, ambient-leaning improvisation. Wewantsounds reissued the album, remastered by King Records in Tokyo with expanded liner notes, in 2025.
