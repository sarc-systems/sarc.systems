Recorded live in February–March 1970 at Tokyo's Station '70 and left unreleased for decades, this material first appeared piecemeal on two P.S.F. Japan CDs, *Call in Question* (1994) and *Live Independence* (1995), before Black Editions gathered it as a deluxe 3LP box in 2022. It documents [Masayuki Takayanagi](/library/research/masayuki-takayanagi/)'s New Direction trio — formed the previous August with bassist Motoharu Yoshizawa and drummer Sabu Toyozumi (Yoshisaburo Toyozumi), joined on one piece by saxophonist Mototeru Takagi — in an early, still-forming stage of the intensely physical, non-responsive playing that would define Takayanagi's later work.
