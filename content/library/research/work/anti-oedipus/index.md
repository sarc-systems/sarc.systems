Published in France in 1972 as *L'Anti-Œdipe*, the first volume of *Capitalism and Schizophrenia* argues that desire is directly productive — a factory, not a theater staging a lack — and that psychoanalysis's Oedipal triangle domesticates and represses desire's genuinely social and historical dimensions. Deleuze and [Guattari](/library/research/felix-guattari/) propose schizoanalysis as an alternative, tracing how capitalism itself works by continuously coding and decoding flows of desire. English translation by Robert Hurley, Mark Seem, and Helen R. Lane (Viking Press, 1977).
