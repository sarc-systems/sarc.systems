Released in 1989 on Selektion, *Acrid Acme (of) P16.D4* gathers [P16.D4](/library/research/p16d4/)'s work across four distinct sections: studio recompositions of 1981 material (Section 1), tracks rearranged from a 1987 festival performance (Section 2), direct-to-tape improvised musique concrète (Section 3), and, closing the album, "Zur Genese der Halbbildung," a 20-minute collaboration between P16.D4 and [Merzbow](/library/research/merzbow/) (Section 4).
