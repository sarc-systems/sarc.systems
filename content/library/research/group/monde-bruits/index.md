Shohei Iwasaki (1962–2005) recorded as Monde Bruits — French for "noise world" — from among the earliest years of Japan's noise scene, releasing through Vanilla Records and G.R.O.S.S. It was Iwasaki who organized [Masami Akita](/library/research/merzbow/)'s first performance as Merzbow, in Osaka, and the two later played together, alongside [C.C.C.C.](/library/research/cccc/)'s Fumio Kosakai and [Solmania](/library/research/solmania/)'s Masahiko Ohno, in the short-lived 1992–94 Osaka supergroup Bustmonster. Iwasaki split the 1996 release *Fragmentagraph* with [Pain Jerk](/library/research/painjerk/), and recorded as Sian with [Aube](/library/research/aube/)'s Akifumi Nakajima. He died in a motorcycle accident in 2005.
