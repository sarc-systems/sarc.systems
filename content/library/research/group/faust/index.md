Assembled in Hamburg in 1970 by producer Uwe Nettelbeck from members of two existing bands, Faust relocated to a converted schoolhouse in rural Wümme to record in relative isolation, building tape-collage and studio-composition techniques directly into the krautrock format across *Faust* (1971), *So Far* (1972), *The Faust Tapes* (1973, sold for 48 pence and reportedly moving over 100,000 copies), and *Faust IV* (1973, recorded at Virgin's English studios). Following a long hiatus, the group's 1994 reunion album *Rien* was produced and mixed by [Jim O'Rourke](/library/research/jim-orourke/).
