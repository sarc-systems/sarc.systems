Formed in New York in 1979, when saxophonists Don Dietrich and Jim Sauter connected through guitarist Donald Miller's radio show on Columbia University's WKCR, Borbetomagus builds an overwhelming, "take-no-prisoners" wall of free-jazz noise, its signature technique pressing two saxophone bells together while playing. Albums including *Borbetomagus* (1980), *Work on What Has Been Spoiled* (1981), and *Barbed Wire Maggots* (1983) established the trio as a formative influence on the American noise underground, including Sonic Youth, and on the Japanese noise scene via acts such as Hijokaidan. The group recorded *[Fish That Sparkling Bubble](/library/research/fish-that-sparkling-bubble/)* (1988) with the Swiss electroacoustic duo [Voice Crack](/library/research/voice-crack/).
