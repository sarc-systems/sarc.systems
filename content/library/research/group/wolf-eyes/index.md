Nate Young began Wolf Eyes as a solo project in Ann Arbor, Michigan, in 1996. [Aaron Dilloway](/library/aaron-dilloway/) joined in 1998, and John Olson — previously of Universal Indians — in 2000, forming the lineup that carried the group through its mid-2000s signing to Sub Pop. Dilloway left in 2005, uninterested in extensive touring, replaced by Mike Connelly (2006–2012) and later Jim Baljo (2013–2017); Young and Olson have continued as a core duo since. The group has released extensively through Olson's own [American Tapes](/library/american-tapes/) and Dilloway's [Hanson Records](/library/hanson-records/), and recorded *Live Frying* (2014, American Tapes) with [Merzbow](/library/merzbow/), who also joined Wolf Eyes and [Richard Pinhas](/library/richard-pinhas/) for the spontaneous live set *[Victoriaville Mai 2011](/library/victoriaville-mai-2011/)*. The group has also collaborated and performed with a wide range of other artists over the years, including [Anthony Braxton](/library/anthony-braxton/), [John Wiese](/library/john-wiese/), and [Smegma](/library/smegma/).
