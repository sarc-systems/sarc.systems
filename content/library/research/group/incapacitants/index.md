Founded in Osaka in 1981 by Toshiji Mikawa as a solo project, Incapacitants remained a one-man vehicle — as heard on 1989's *[Repo](/library/research/repo/)* — until Fumio Kosakai, following the group's relocation to Tokyo, became a permanent member around 1991, making Incapacitants the duo it has been ever since. Together they pursue what Kosakai calls "hard noise" — a term borrowed from jazz — built from feedback, vocals, and electronics toward a "pure" noise deliberately stripped of musical intention, released chiefly through Alchemy Records. Kosakai also occasionally contributed to [C.C.C.C.](/library/research/cccc/), formed in Osaka in 1989, and both Mikawa and Kosakai later became regular members of the noise group Hijokaidan. Day jobs — Mikawa at a bank, Kosakai in a government office — have limited the duo's international touring.
