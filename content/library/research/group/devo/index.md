Formed in Akron, Ohio, in 1973 by brothers Mark and Bob Mothersbaugh and Gerald and Bob Casale, Devo built its music and stage persona around a satirical concept of human "de-evolution," fusing rock instrumentation with electronics, deadpan science-fiction imagery, and mordant social commentary. [Brian Eno](/library/research/brian-eno/) produced the group's 1978 debut, *[Q: Are We Not Men? A: We Are Devo!](/library/research/q-are-we-not-men-a-we-are-devo/)*, for Warner Bros., helping translate the band's confrontational live sound into a record now regarded as a founding document of American new wave.
