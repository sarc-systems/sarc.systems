Founded by Roger Schönauer and Ewald Weber, P16.D4 fused dadaist irrationality with industrial and expressionist tone across a run of releases on the Selektion label — the debut *Kühe in 1/2 Trauer* (1984), the deconstructed-remix album *Distruct* (1984, joined by Stefan E. Schmidt), and the double album *Nichts Niemand Nirgends Nie* (1986), moving further into musique concrète and electronic improvisation. The group's revolving membership included Ralf Wehowsky, Gerd Poppe, and Achim Szepanski, who later founded the label [Mille Plateaux](/library/research/mille-plateaux/). P16.D4 recorded directly with [Merzbow](/library/research/merzbow/) on *[Acrid Acme (of) P16.D4](/library/research/acrid-acme-of-p16d4/)* (1989).
