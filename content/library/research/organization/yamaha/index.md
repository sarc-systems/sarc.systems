Founded by Torakusu Yamaha as Nippon Gakki Co., Ltd. in Hamamatsu, Japan, in 1887, initially building reed organs; the company took the Yamaha Corporation name in 1987, its centennial. Stanford University licensed [John Chowning](/library/research/john-chowning/)'s newly discovered FM synthesis algorithm to Yamaha starting in 1973, and Yamaha built that license into its digital synthesis engine — most famously the [DX7](/library/research/yamaha-dx7/) and its flagship counterpart, the [DX1](/library/research/yamaha-dx1/).
