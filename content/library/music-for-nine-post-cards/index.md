Hiroshi Yoshimura's 1982 debut, home-recorded on keyboard and Fender Rhodes and released on Sound Process — the label he co-founded with Satoshi Ashikawa — inaugurating the Wave Notation series and helping define kankyo ongaku, or environmental music.
