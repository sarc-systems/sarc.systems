Valentino Braitenberg (1926–2011) trained as a psychiatrist and neurologist before Eduardo Caianiello invited him to set up a biocybernetics research group at the University of Naples in 1958. From 1968 until his 1994 retirement he was a founding director of the Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics in Tübingen, leading its department on the structure and function of natural nerve-nets. His 1984 book *Vehicles: Experiments in Synthetic Psychology* built simple sensorimotor thought-experiments into a foundational case for lifelike behavior emerging from minimal coupled parts.
