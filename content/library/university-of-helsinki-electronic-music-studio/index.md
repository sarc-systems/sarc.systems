Proposed to musicology professor Erik Tawaststjerna by students in 1962 and built out technically by Erkki Kurenniemi, the University of Helsinki's electronic music studio began modestly — tape recorders, oscillators, and a spring reverb in the corner of a department office — before Kurenniemi built ten original instruments and some thirty electroacoustic works there through 1975. It remains the oldest continuously operating electronic music studio in the Nordic countries.
