Albert Ayler (1936–1970) recast free jazz around raw tone, folk and march melody fragments, and collective improvisation. His trio session with bassist Gary Peacock and drummer Sunny Murray, recorded July 10, 1964, became *Spiritual Unity* — the first jazz recording on Bernard Stollman's newly founded ESP-Disk' label.
