Knut Wiggen (1927–2016) planned and led Elektronmusikstudion (EMS) in Stockholm from 1964 to 1975, where he created MusicBox, an early object-oriented simulation language for spatial electronic composition, and used it to compose works including *Sommarmorgon* (1972) and *Massa* (1974). He had earlier chaired the Swedish new-music society Fylkingen from 1959 to 1969.
