John von Neumann (1903–1957) published, by age 19, a new definition of the ordinal numbers that superseded [Georg Cantor's](/library/georg-cantor/) own, and his doctoral thesis produced an axiomatization of Cantor's set theory, introducing the axiom of foundation and the notion of "class" to close off the paradoxes naive set theory had run into. He studied under [David Hilbert](/library/david-hilbert/) at Göttingen in the 1920s, later engaging directly with Kurt Gödel's incompleteness theorems, and went on to found the mathematical theory of self-reproducing automata, lay foundations for game theory, and shape the stored-program architecture underlying almost all subsequent computing.
