Hildegard Westerkamp (b. 1946, Osnabrück) emigrated to Canada in 1968 and, after
studying music at the University of British Columbia, joined R. Murray
Schafer's World Soundscape Project at Simon Fraser University in the early
1970s. She produced and hosted the radio program *Soundwalking* on Vancouver
Co-operative Radio (1978–79), composed *Kits Beach Soundwalk*, and helped
found the World Forum for Acoustic Ecology in 1993, editing its journal
*Soundscape* from 2000 to 2012.
