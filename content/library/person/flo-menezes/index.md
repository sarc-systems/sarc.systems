Brazilian composer Flo Menezes (b. 1962) spent 1986–1990 as a guest composer at Cologne's [Studio für elektronische Musik des WDR](/library/studio-fur-elektronische-musik-des-wdr/), then worked at Italy's Centro di Sonologia Computazionale near Bologna in 1991 before founding [Studio PANaroma](/library/studio-panaroma/) in São Paulo in July 1994 — explicitly modeled on the Cologne studio, with its name carried over from a private studio he had run in Italy. He later taught analysis at [Karlheinz Stockhausen's](/library/karlheinz-stockhausen/) courses in Germany.
