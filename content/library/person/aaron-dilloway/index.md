Aaron Dilloway (b. May 13, 1976, Brighton, Michigan) founded [Hanson Records](/library/hanson-records/) in 1994 and joined [Wolf Eyes](/library/wolf-eyes/) in 1998, working with tape loops and shortwave radio within the group until 2005, when he left rather than continue extensive touring. Late in 2004 he moved to Nepal, recording prolifically — his own material, field recordings, and intercepted Nepalese radio broadcasts — before returning to the United States, settling in Oberlin, Ohio, in 2007. His solo catalog includes *Modern Jester* (2012, Hanson) and *The Gag File* (2017, Dais Records).
