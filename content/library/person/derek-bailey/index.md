Derek Bailey (1930–2005) was central to the development of free improvisation and non-idiomatic playing, co-founding the Music Improvisation Company with Evan Parker, Jamie Muir, and Hugh Davies in 1968, and Incus Records with Parker, Tony Oxley, and Michael Walters in 1970 — the first musician-owned independent record label in England, launched with the trio recording *The Topography of the Lungs* alongside Parker and Han Bennink. From 1987 to 1994 he organized the ad hoc Company Weeks, bringing together improvisers across disparate traditions.
