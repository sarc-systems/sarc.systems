Félix Guattari (1930–1992) was a French psychoanalyst and militant political theorist, based for most of his career at the experimental La Borde clinic outside Paris, where he developed institutional-psychotherapy practices that broke with orthodox Freudian and Lacanian technique. His collaboration with [Gilles Deleuze](/library/gilles-deleuze/), beginning after the events of May 1968, produced *[Anti-Oedipus](/library/anti-oedipus/)* (1972) and *[A Thousand Plateaus](/library/a-thousand-plateaus/)* (1980) — together *Capitalism and Schizophrenia* — introducing schizoanalysis, the rhizome, and the body without organs as tools for thinking about desire, multiplicity, and non-hierarchical organization outside psychoanalytic and structuralist orthodoxy.
