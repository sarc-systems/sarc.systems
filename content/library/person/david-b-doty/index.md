David B. Doty began composing in just intonation in 1970, inspired in part by the work of Harry Partch, and went on to write *The Just Intonation Primer* (1993) as a practical introduction meant to prepare readers for more advanced texts, chief among them Partch's own *Genesis of a Music*.
