Jim O'Rourke (b. 1969, Chicago) emerged from that city's experimental and improvised music scene in the late 1980s, an early member of Daniel Burke's [Illusion of Safety](/library/illusion-of-safety/) and co-composer of its 1992 album *Probe*, working across labels including Sound of Pig, Staalplaat, and Skin Graft before forming a long duo with David Grubbs as [Gastr del Sol](/library/gastr-del-sol/) (1993–1998). He joined Sonic Youth as an official member from 1999 to 2005, and from 1997 was a member of the improvised computer-music trio Fenn O'Berg alongside Fennesz and [Peter Rehberg](/library/peter-rehberg/). His solo catalog splits between song-oriented Drag City albums — *Bad Timing* (1997), *Eureka* (1999), *Insignificance* (2001) — and an ongoing Bandcamp archival series, Steamroom, built largely around a [Serge modular synthesizer](/library/serge-modular-synthesizer/). As a producer he mixed Wilco's *Yankee Hotel Foxtrot* and produced their Grammy-winning *A Ghost Is Born*, produced [John Fahey](/library/john-fahey/)'s 1997 album *Womblife* during Fahey's late-career turn toward the avant-garde, and produced and mixed [Faust](/library/faust/)'s 1994 reunion album *Rien*. Since 2017 he has restored and mastered [Roland Kayn](/library/roland-kayn/)'s cybernetic-music catalog for its ongoing posthumous release, and has recorded duo albums with [Kassel Jaeger](/library/kassel-jaeger/) (*Wakes on Cerulean*, 2017; *In Cobalt Aura Sleeps*, 2020) and [PBK](/library/pbk/) (*Unidentified Again*, 2015). [Tony Conrad](/library/tony-conrad/) contributed to [Gastr del Sol](/library/gastr-del-sol/)'s *Upgrade & Afterlife* (1996), and O'Rourke has also recorded with [Voice Crack](/library/voice-crack/) and [Günter Müller](/library/gunter-muller/) (*Table Chair and Hatstand*, 1997), [John Duncan](/library/john-duncan/) (*Yeah*, 2014), and [Derek Bailey](/library/derek-bailey/), appearing on his 1998 album *Playbacks*. Further credited collaborations include [Eddie Prévost](/library/eddie-prevost/) and [Michael Prime](/library/michael-allen-z-prime/) (*Alpha Lemur Echo Two*, 2001), multiple mixing and remix credits for [Organum](/library/organum/) (1990–2004), a contribution to [Nurse With Wound](/library/nurse-with-wound/)'s *Angry Eelectric Finger* (2004), a run of collaborations with [Merzbow](/library/merzbow/) spanning a 1997 *Scumtron* remix to 2015's *Flying Basket*, an orchestral-arrangement remix contributed (alongside [Violent Onsen Geisha](/library/violent-onsen-geisha/) and others) to [Microstoria](/library/microstoria/)'s 1997 remix album *Reprovisers*, and the 2000 tribute album *Tribute to [Masayuki Takayanagi](/library/masayuki-takayanagi/)*. O'Rourke relocated to Japan around 2000, where he continues to score films and record with Eiko Ishibashi.
