François J. Bonnet (b. 1981, Limoges), recording as Kassel Jaeger, joined [GRM](/library/grm/) — the Groupe de Recherches Musicales — as a member in 2007 and became its director in 2018, also writing and editing GRM's Spectres book series. He has recorded two duo albums with [Jim O'Rourke](/library/jim-orourke/), *Wakes on Cerulean* (2017) and *In Cobalt Aura Sleeps* (2020).
