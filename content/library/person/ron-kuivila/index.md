Ron Kuivila is an American composer working in live electronics and experimental sound, trained under Alvin Lucier at Wesleyan University, where he later joined the faculty himself. His collaboration with fellow Lucier student Nicolas Collins produced the 1982 split release *Going Out With Slow Smoke*.
