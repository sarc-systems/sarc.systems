Serge Tcherepnin (b. 1941, Issy-les-Moulineaux, near Paris) is the third generation of a Russian composing family — his father was Alexander Tcherepnin, his grandfather Nikolai. He studied under Leon Kirchner and Billy Jim Layton at Harvard from 1958 to 1963, then in Europe from 1966 to 1968 with Pierre Boulez, Herbert Eimert, and [Karlheinz Stockhausen](/library/karlheinz-stockhausen/). Teaching at CalArts under Morton Subotnick's direction, he encountered [Don Buchla](/library/don-buchla/)'s early modular synthesizers, which led him to design his own system; he founded [Serge Modular Music Systems](/library/serge-modular-music-systems/) in 1974, building instruments from locations around Los Angeles until the company closed in 1986. In 2018 he became Chief Innovation Officer at Random*Source, the German manufacturer now producing new Serge modules.
