Robert Moog (1934–2005) built theremin kits through his R.A. Moog Company in the 1950s before introducing a voltage-controlled subtractive synthesizer, played from a keyboard, in 1964 — work that gave the company its later name, Moog Music, in 1971. He met composer-inventor [Raymond Scott](/library/raymond-scott/) in the 1950s and designed circuits for him through the 1960s, later naming Scott an important influence on his own work.
