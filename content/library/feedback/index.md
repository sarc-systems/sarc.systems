A loop in which a system's own output is measured and returned as input, altering its subsequent behavior. Negative feedback drives a system toward a stable target — the operating principle of a [governor](/library/governor/), a thermostat, or a [servo](/library/servo/) — while positive feedback amplifies a deviation rather than correcting it. [Norbert Wiener](/library/norbert-wiener/) made feedback the organizing concept of cybernetics itself in 1948, naming the field from the Greek *kybernetes* ("steersman," or "governor").
