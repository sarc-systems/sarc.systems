Wave Notation was the series Satoshi Ashikawa curated on Sound Process, the label he founded in 1982, to release *kankyō ongaku* (environmental music) conceived as musical landscape rather than foreground listening. It comprised three LPs: Hiroshi Yoshimura's *Music for Nine Post Cards* (1982), Ashikawa's own *Still Way* (1982), and Satsuki Shibano's *Erik Satie (France 1866–1925)* (1984) — the series ended with Ashikawa's early death.
