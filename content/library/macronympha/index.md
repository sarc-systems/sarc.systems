Macronympha was formed in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, in 1990 by Joseph Roemer and Rodger Stella, building dense harsh-noise collage from metal junk, analogue electronics, and looped and layered tape — one of the longer-running acts in the American cassette-noise underground that took shape alongside labels documenting the genre through small-run releases. Stella left the project in 2007; Roemer has continued it since, with occasional guest appearances from Tim Oliveira (Stimbox) and Dominick Fernow (Prurient), releasing primarily through his own Mother Savage Noise Productions.
