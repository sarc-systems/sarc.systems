Gil Mellé (1931–2004) began as a post-bop baritone saxophonist on Blue Note and Prestige before moving into film and television scoring in the 1960s. His 1970 theme for *Night Gallery* was the first all-electronic main title for a TV series, and his 1971 score for *The Andromeda Strain* became the first all-synthesizer score for a feature film, across a career of more than 125 film and television scores.
