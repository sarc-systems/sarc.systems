Formed in London in 1985 — first performing that July at the West Square Festival — Morphogenesis explores improvised noise music through unconventional instruments and self-built sound-making devices. Its founding lineup of Roger Sutherland (a former member of the Scratch Orchestra), Adam Bohman, Ron Briefel, Clive Hall, Fred Sansom, and [Michael Prime](/library/research/michael-allen-z-prime/), who developed techniques for using the bioelectrical fields of plants as a sound source and occasionally performed with [Organum](/library/research/organum/), shifted over the following decade: Clive Graham, an occasional collaborator with [Nurse With Wound](/library/research/nurse-with-wound/), replaced Sansom in 1989, and Andy Cordery joined for two years in the early 1990s.
