Kohei Gomi has recorded as Pain Jerk since the 1980s, one of the more prolific and influential noise artists of the 1990s and one of the leading figures — alongside [Merzbow](/library/research/merzbow/) and Kazumoto Endo — of noise's "dynamic" style: dense, loop-driven pieces built from cycling and layering rather than sustained drone. Gomi owns the noise label AMP, through which much of his catalog, including *Spitfire* (1997) and *Mission Invisible – Kill the Poor* (1997), has appeared.

Gomi split the 1996 release *Fragmentagraph* with [Monde Bruits](/library/research/monde-bruits/), and recorded with [Aube](/library/research/aube/)'s Akifumi Nakajima as Meiji Jingu and, joined further by [Solmania](/library/research/solmania/)'s Masahiko Ohno and Toshiji Mikawa of Incapacitants, as the four-way Atsuta Jingu. With Mikawa and [C.C.C.C.](/library/research/cccc/)'s Fumio Kosakai, Gomi also forms the noise supergroup Gomikawa Fumio, whose releases include *Return of Gomikawa Fumio* (2002, Alchemy Records) and *The Rise and Fall of Gomikawa Fumio* (2021).
