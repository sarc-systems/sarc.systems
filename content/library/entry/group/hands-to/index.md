Hands To was one of the names [Jeph Jerman](/library/research/jeph-jerman/) recorded and performed under from the mid-1980s, alongside sampler-and-tape work as City of Worms and noise-rock improvisation as Big Joey — building pieces from found natural objects, crude homebuilt devices, and tape manipulation rather than conventional or electronic instruments. Releases included *Fingers Breath* (Sound of Pig Music, 1987) and *Do Not Touch Them* (Big Body Parts, 1987). Jerman eventually stopped using the Hands To name, continuing his sound work under his own name and with numerous collaborators.
