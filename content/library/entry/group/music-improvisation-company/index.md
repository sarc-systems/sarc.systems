Active in London from 1968 to 1971, the Music Improvisation Company paired [Derek Bailey](/library/research/derek-bailey/)'s guitar and [Evan Parker](/library/research/evan-parker/)'s soprano saxophone and amplified autoharp with [Hugh Davies](/library/research/hugh-davies/)'s live electronics and organ and Jamie Muir's percussion and painting — Bailey described the electronics as extending the music "both forwards and backwards." Recordings from a mono session on July 4, 1969, and a stereo session on June 18, 1970, were gathered on the group's self-titled *1968–1971* (1976), released on [Incus Records](/library/research/incus-records/).
