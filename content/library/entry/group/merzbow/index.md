Merzbow is a Japanese noise project founded in Tokyo in 1979 by [Masami Akita](/library/research/masami-akita/) and [Kiyoshi Mizutani](/library/research/kiyoshi-mizutani/), high-school friends who had already been playing improvised rock together — its name taken from Kurt Schwitters' *Merzbau* to signal a junk-art, Dada-inflected sensibility. Mizutani performed on Merzbow's earliest tape releases and live shows through the 1980s before leaving after the project's 1989 European tour to pursue a solo career; Akita has continued Merzbow alone since, its studio output now exceeding 300 full-length releases and widely regarded as among the most important and prolific bodies of work in noise music.
