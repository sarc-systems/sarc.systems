Formed in 1982 by Gary Mundy as part of the first wave of British power electronics alongside Whitehouse and Sutcliffe Jugend, Ramleh built harsh, feedback-driven noise from vocals, guitar, and electronics before disbanding in 1984. The group reformed in 1989 with Philip Best (ex-Whitehouse, Consumer Electronics) and Anthony di Franco, gradually absorbing rock structure and drums — evolving toward a noise-rock sound often compared to Skullflower (Mundy's other group) and the Butthole Surfers — before a second hiatus around 1997 and a reunion in 2009 that continues today with di Franco, drummer Stuart Dennison, and Martyn Watts. Mundy's own label, [Broken Flag](/library/research/broken-flag/), was launched during the band's first hiatus specifically to keep Ramleh's catalog in print.
