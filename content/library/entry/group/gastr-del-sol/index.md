David Grubbs formed Gastr del Sol in Chicago in 1991 out of the dissolving post-hardcore band Bastro; when the rhythm section left to join Tortoise after 1993's debut *The Serpentine Similar*, [Jim O'Rourke](/library/research/jim-orourke/) became Grubbs's sole ongoing creative partner. Across *Crookt, Crackt, or Fly* (1994), *Upgrade & Afterlife* (1996), and *Camoufleur* (1998), the duo moved between spare acoustic-guitar composition and chamber-scale arrangement, drawing in outside contributors including [Tony Conrad](/library/research/tony-conrad/), Kevin Drumm, Günter Müller, and Oval's [Markus Popp](/library/research/markus-popp/). The group dissolved after *Camoufleur*, each principal continuing separately into rock, pop, and experimental work.
