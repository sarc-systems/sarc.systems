Formed in the early 1980s by brothers Richard and Philip Rupenus, The New Blockaders (TNB) reject the term "music" for their work, framing it instead as "anti-music" — a stance of deliberate sonic nihilism opposed to conventional notions of music, art, and even anti-art. Their debut, *[Changez les Blockeurs](/library/research/changez-les-blockeurs/)* (1982), built from junk-metal noise, scrapes, and disorienting found sound, is widely cited (Ron Lessard of RRRecords called it "the first true noise record of the modern era") as a foundational document of noise music, directly influencing [Merzbow](/library/research/merzbow/), Hijokaidan, and [Incapacitants](/library/research/incapacitants/) among the emerging Japanese noise scene, as well as [Nurse With Wound](/library/research/nurse-with-wound/), Coil, and [Sonic Youth](/library/research/sonic-youth/). TNB also collaborated directly with [Organum](/library/research/organum/)'s David Jackman on *Pulp* (1984).
