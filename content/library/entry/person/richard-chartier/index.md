Richard Chartier founded Line, a label documenting compositional and installation work by sound artists exploring minimalism, and released Steve Roden's *Forms of Paper* (2001) on it — an album credited as the first "lowercase" record. He and Roden went on to collaborate directly, alongside Bernhard Günter, on *(For Morton Feldman)* the following year.
