Michel Waisvisz (1949–2008) joined STEIM in 1969 and directed the studio from 1981 to 2008, shaping its focus on live, gestural electronic performance. In 1984 he introduced The Hands, a pair of sensor-equipped gloves letting arm, finger, and tilt movement control electronic sound — one of the earliest MIDI-based gestural instruments, built the year after the standard's introduction and developed across three versions over nearly a quarter-century.
