Masami Akita (b. 1956, Tokyo) began playing improvised rock with high-school friend [Kiyoshi Mizutani](/library/research/kiyoshi-mizutani/) before founding [Merzbow](/library/research/merzbow/) with him in 1979, taking the project's name from Kurt Schwitters' *Merzbau* to signal a junk-art, Dada-inflected approach to noise. That year he also founded the cassette label Lowest Music & Arts to trade tapes with other underground artists, releasing Merzbow's earliest recordings, including the debut tape *Metal Acoustic Music*, through it. As Merzbow's sole constant member since Mizutani's 1989 departure, Akita has driven the project's studio output past 300 full-length releases — a discography spanning early junk-percussion noise, harsh-noise LPs of the 1990s such as *Pulse Demon* (1996), and the fifty-disc *Merzbox* retrospective (2000) — while also recording extensively under his own name and in collaboration with musicians including drummer Balázs Pándi and saxophonist Mats Gustafsson from 2009 onward.
