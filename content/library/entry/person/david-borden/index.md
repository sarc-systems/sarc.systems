David Borden (b. 1938) studied composition at the Eastman School of Music and Harvard University before founding [Mother Mallard's Portable Masterpiece Company](/library/research/mother-mallard/) in Ithaca, New York in 1969, with the direct support of [Robert Moog](/library/research/robert-moog/) — Borden was among the first musicians to perform on Moog's Minimoog. The ensemble's repertoire drew on the minimalism of Terry Riley, Steve Reich, and Philip Glass, a lineage Borden extended in his own twelve-part cycle *The Continuing Story of Counterpoint* (1976–87). He went on to found Cornell University's Digital Music Program, later the Cornell Electroacoustic Music Center.
