Sachiko Matsubara, performing as Sachiko M, has worked as a sampler player since 1994, moving from the cut-up and plunderphonic sampling approaches of her early career to a radically reduced method she originated in 1998: playing only a sampler's own internal test tones rather than any recorded material. Her 2000 solo release *Sine Wave Solo*, built entirely from these tones, drew international attention to the technique and to Tokyo's onkyo scene of minimalist, silence-based improvisation, of which she was a central figure alongside [Otomo Yoshihide](/library/research/otomo-yoshihide/) — with whom she played in Ground Zero — and Toshimaru Nakamura.
