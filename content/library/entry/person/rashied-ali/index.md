Rashied Ali (1935–2009) became John Coltrane's sole drummer in 1966 after McCoy Tyner and Elvin Jones left the group, playing on Coltrane's final recordings including the free duets of *Interstellar Space* (1967). In 1972 he recorded *Duo Exchange* with tenor saxophonist Frank Lowe, released the following year as the inaugural album on Ali and Lowe's own Survival Records.
