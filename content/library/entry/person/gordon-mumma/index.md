Gordon Mumma is an American composer and horn player who, after working alongside Robert Ashley, David Behrman, and Alvin Lucier in the instrumental performances of the ONCE festivals, co-founded the Sonic Arts Union with them in 1966, building his own live-electronic circuitry for the group's performances.
