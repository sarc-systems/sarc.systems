Tony Conrad (1940–2016) was an American violinist, filmmaker, and composer who joined La Monte Young's Theatre of Eternal Music from 1964 to 1966, playing sustained just-intonation drones on amplified violin. In 1966 he made *The Flicker*, a landmark of structural cinema built entirely from alternating black and white frames, and he carried his drone practice forward into his own music, most notably the 1973 collaboration with Faust, *Outside the Dream Syndicate*.
