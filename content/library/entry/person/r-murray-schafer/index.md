R. Murray Schafer (1933–2021) founded the World Soundscape Project at Simon
Fraser University in the late 1960s, coining "soundscape" and "schizophonia"
and arguing for listening to the total acoustic environment as a form of
composition. His 1977 book *The Tuning of the World* — reissued in 1994 as
*The Soundscape* — became the foundational text of acoustic ecology.
