Chu Ishikawa (1966–2017) was a Japanese composer who founded the industrial music groups Der Eisenrost and Zeitlich Vergelter before scoring [Tetsuo: The Iron Man](/library/research/tetsuo-iron-man/) (1989), his first film work, for director Shinya Tsukamoto. Told to build the score from "only the sound of metal," Ishikawa produced long, dense industrial-percussion pieces that became inseparable from the film's identity, and went on to score Tsukamoto's *Tetsuo II: Body Hammer* (1992), *Tokyo Fist* (1995), *Bullet Ballet* (1998), and *Tetsuo: The Bullet Man* (2010), as well as films by Takashi Miike including *Fudoh: The New Generation* (1996) and *Dead or Alive 2: Birds* (2000).
