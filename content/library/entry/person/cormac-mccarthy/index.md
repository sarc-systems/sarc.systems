Cormac McCarthy (1933–2023) was an American novelist, author of *Blood Meridian*, *All the Pretty Horses*, *No Country for Old Men*, and *The Road*. For more than two decades he was a fixture of the [Santa Fe Institute](/library/entry/santa-fe-institute/), where he served as a board member and senior fellow and spent much of his time among its scientists, editing their writing and pursuing interests in mathematics, physics, non-human intelligence, and consciousness. Those concerns surface in his last novels, *The Passenger* and *Stella Maris* (2022), and in a rare piece of nonfiction, the essay [The Kekulé Problem](/library/entry/the-kekule-problem/), on the unconscious and the origins of language.
