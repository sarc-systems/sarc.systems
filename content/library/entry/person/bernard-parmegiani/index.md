Bernard Parmegiani (1927–2013) studied mime under Jacques Lecoq from 1957 to 1961, an apprenticeship he later credited as important to his sense of gesture and timing as a composer, before moving into sound engineering. He joined the [GRM](/library/research/grm/) in 1959 for a two-year masterclass shortly after its founding by [Pierre Schaeffer](/library/research/pierre-schaeffer/), then took charge of the Music/Image unit for French state television (ORTF), collaborating there with composers including Iannis Xenakis. His acousmatic works of the period include *Violostries* (1964), for violin and tape, and the concert pieces *Capture éphémère* and *L'Enfer* (1967–1972), culminating in his best-known work, *[De Natura Sonorum](/library/research/de-natura-sonorum/)* (1975). He left the GRM in 1992 to establish his own studio in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence.
