Gilles Deleuze (1925–1995) taught philosophy at the University of Paris VIII, Vincennes, for most of his career. *[Difference and Repetition](/library/research/difference-and-repetition/)* (1968), his doctoral thesis, argues that difference is prior to identity and repetition productive rather than merely reproductive — the conceptual groundwork for his later collaborations with [Félix Guattari](/library/research/felix-guattari/). Beginning after May 1968, that partnership produced the two volumes of *Capitalism and Schizophrenia*, *[Anti-Oedipus](/library/research/anti-oedipus/)* (1972) and *[A Thousand Plateaus](/library/research/a-thousand-plateaus/)* (1980), reworking psychoanalysis, political economy, and the organization of thought itself around concepts of desire, multiplicity, and the rhizome.
