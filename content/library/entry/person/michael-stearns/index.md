Michael Stearns (b. 1948) is an American composer working in electronic and ambient music since the early 1970s, alongside Steve Roach and Robert Rich a founding figure of the Californian space-music scene. Beyond his solo albums he built The Beam, a twelve-foot aluminum shaft strung with piano wire for generating deep low frequencies, and became a prolific film and large-format sound designer, scoring Ron Fricke's *Baraka* among many others.
