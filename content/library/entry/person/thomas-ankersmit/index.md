Born 1979 in Leiden, Thomas Ankersmit has worked primarily on the [Serge modular synthesizer](/library/research/serge-modular-synthesizer/) since 1998, first through live performance and installation work and, from 2010, on releases for PAN, Touch, and Ash International. His work explores physical and psychoacoustic phenomena — sound reflections, infrasonic vibration, otoacoustic emission — often through deliberate misuse of his equipment, pushing feedback and signal disruption into the compositional material itself. He recorded *Weerzin/Oscillators and Guitars* (2005) with [Jim O'Rourke](/library/research/jim-orourke/), and has also collaborated with Phill Niblock, Valerio Tricoli, and Kevin Drumm.
