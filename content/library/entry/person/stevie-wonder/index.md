Born May 13, 1950, in Saginaw, Michigan, Stevie Wonder's "classic period" (1972–1974) was defined by his pioneering use of [TONTO](/library/research/tonto/), in collaboration with engineer-producers Malcolm Cecil and Robert Margouleff, giving *Music of My Mind*, *Talking Book*, *Innervisions*, and *Fulfillingness' First Finale* their layered synthesizer textures. Separately, Wonder became an early tester and public champion of [Ray Kurzweil](/library/research/ray-kurzweil/)'s Kurzweil Reading Machine after seeing it demonstrated on a news broadcast, a relationship that led him to co-found [Kurzweil Music Systems](/library/research/kurzweil/) with Kurzweil in 1982.
