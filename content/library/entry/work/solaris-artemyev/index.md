Eduard Artemyev's score for Andrei Tarkovsky's *Solaris* (1972) is built on the pioneering ANS synthesizer, centered on somber variations of J.S. Bach's Chorale Prelude in F Minor set against dissonant, formless drone — the first of Artemyev's three Tarkovsky scores, preceding *The Mirror* and *Stalker*.
