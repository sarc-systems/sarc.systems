Recorded in Antwerp on September 30, 1995, during Mal Waldron's 70th birthday tour, *The Mighty Warriors* documents [Waldron](/library/research/mal-waldron/) and [Steve Lacy](/library/research/steve-lacy/)'s quartet with Reggie Workman on bass and [Andrew Cyrille](/library/research/andrew-cyrille/) on drums, working through a set including "Epistrophy," "Monk's Dream," and Waldron's "Snake Out." Released in 2024 by Elemental Music as a limited numbered pressing, with liner notes drawn from interviews with Evan Parker, Vijay Iyer, David Liebman, Jane Bunnett, and David Virelles, among others.
