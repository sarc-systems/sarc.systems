Commissioned by Luciano Berio for Pierre Boulez's inaugural concert series at the Centre Pompidou and premiered there in 1977, John Chowning's 17-minute tape piece *Stria* built both its FM carrier-to-modulator ratios and its own tuning system — a "pseudo-octave" of 1:1.618 rather than 1:2 — entirely from the golden ratio and the Fibonacci sequence, realized at Stanford's CCRMA in SAIL on a DEC PDP-10.
