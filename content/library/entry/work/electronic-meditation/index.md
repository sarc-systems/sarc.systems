Recorded in a rented Berlin factory space in October 1969 on a two-track Revox and released on Ohr in June 1970, *Electronic Meditation* is [Tangerine Dream](/library/research/tangerine-dream/)'s debut album and the only record to capture the trio lineup of Edgar Froese (guitars, organ, piano, tape, and effects), [Klaus Schulze](/library/research/klaus-schulze/) (drums and percussion), and [Conrad Schnitzler](/library/research/conrad-schnitzler/) (cello and violin), with uncredited additional playing from organist Jimmy Jackson and flautist Thomas Keyserling. An entirely instrumental record built from tape manipulation and free-rock instrumentation rather than synthesizers, it is the most straightforwardly krautrock-adjacent album in the group's catalogue — both Schulze and Schnitzler left after its release, Schulze to co-found Ash Ra Tempel and Schnitzler to form Kluster with Dieter Moebius and Hans-Joachim Roedelius.
