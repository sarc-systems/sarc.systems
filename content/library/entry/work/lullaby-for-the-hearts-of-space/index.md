Released on Hearts of Space Records in 1980, *Lullaby for the Hearts of Space* collects two spontaneous, improvised pieces Kevin Fortune performed live on his own customized "Mighty Serge" modular synthesizer — the first recorded for a Hearts of Space radio special at KPFA-FM that March, the second captured live during a class connected to Emilie Conrad-Da'Oud's Continuum movement work.
