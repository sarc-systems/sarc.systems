Recorded and mixed between February and May 1995 at [Merzbow](/library/research/merzbow/)'s ZSF Produkt Studio, *Magnesia Nova* layers overloaded, pulsating synthesizers and feedback into dense noise collages, its track titles all drawn from the writings of 17th-century polymath Athanasius Kircher — part of the album's wider interest in intersections between Greek and Japanese civilization. Urashima's 2022 release was the album's first-ever vinyl pressing, a limited double LP with a 20-minute previously unreleased bonus track from the original sessions.
