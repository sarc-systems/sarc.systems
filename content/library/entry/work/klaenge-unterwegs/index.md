Composed as Opus 28 at the Siemens-Studio für elektronische Musik in Munich, "Klänge Unterwegs" ("Wayfaring Sounds") is an early electronic work by Herbert Brün, first issued alongside "Anepigraphe" on a 1962 educational LP introducing electronic sound.
