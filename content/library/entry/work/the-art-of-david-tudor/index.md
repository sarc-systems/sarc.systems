New World Records' seven-disc box set is the first comprehensive survey of David Tudor's work as a composer, tracing his arc from his first composition, *Bandoneon! (A Combine)*, through his long collaboration scoring for the Merce Cunningham Dance Company, recordings made in the Pepsi Pavilion at Expo '70 in Osaka, two extended binaural recordings of *Rainforest IV* with Composers Inside Electronics, and a complete hour-long 1992 performance of *Neural Network Plus* with Takehisa Kosugi on live electronics — one of Tudor's first ventures into computer music.
