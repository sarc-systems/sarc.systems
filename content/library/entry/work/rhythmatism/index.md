Recorded in late 1975 at Mustevic Sound Studios and self-released by drummer Steve Reid in 1976 in an edition of 1,000, *Rhythmatism* is a five-track spiritual-jazz record featuring Arthur Blythe, Charles Tyler, and David Wertman among others.
