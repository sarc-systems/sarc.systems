Released in 1972 on the French label Shanti, *Persian Surgery Dervishes* documents two extended solo electric organ improvisations by [Terry Riley](/library/research/terry-riley/) — "Performance One," recorded in Los Angeles on April 18, 1971, and "Performance Two," recorded in Paris on May 24, 1972, at a concert organized and recorded by Shandar at the Théâtre de la Musique. Each performance fills a full LP side pair, built from Riley's tape-delay-inflected organ lines shaped by his ongoing study of Hindustani classical singing under Pandit Pran Nath. [Patrick Gleeson](/library/research/patrick-gleeson/) engineered the recording.
