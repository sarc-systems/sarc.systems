A 1987 C-60 cassette released on Sound of Pig Music, *Fingers Breath* documents [Hands To](/library/research/hands-to/) — [Jeph Jerman](/library/research/jeph-jerman/)'s project built from found natural objects, homemade devices, and tape — during the same prolific mid-1980s stretch that produced *Do Not Touch Them*.
