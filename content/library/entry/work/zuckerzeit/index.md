Recorded after Dieter Moebius and Hans-Joachim Roedelius relocated [Cluster](/library/research/cluster/) to the rural village of Forst and produced again by [Conny Plank](/library/research/conny-plank/), *Zuckerzeit* marked a sharp turn toward shorter, more rhythmic, synthesizer-and-drum-machine-driven pieces — a lighter, more melodic sound than the group's earlier drone albums, arriving the same year the pair began recording as [Harmonia](/library/research/harmonia/) with Michael Rother, and anticipating the two collaborative albums they would go on to make with Brian Eno later in the decade.
