Realized at UC San Diego's electronic music studio between 1967 and 1970, in the years just after Pauline Oliveros co-founded the university's music department, *A Little Noise in the System* was later collected on her twelve-disc retrospective box set *Reverberations: Tape & Electronic Music 1960–1970*.
