Published by English Universities Press in 1959, *Cybernetics and Management*
was Stafford Beer's first book and the founding text of management
cybernetics — proposing that Norbert Wiener's science of control and
communication applied directly to the "science of effective organization,"
years before Beer's *Brain of the Firm* (1972) formalized the Viable System
Model or his Chilean Project Cybersyn put the ideas into practice.
