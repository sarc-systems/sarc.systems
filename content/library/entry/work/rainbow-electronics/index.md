Originally released in 1990 on Alchemy Records, *Rainbow Electronics* condenses roughly 21 hours of material [Merzbow](/library/research/merzbow/) recorded between 1987 and 1990 into four parts spanning about 74 minutes, moving from slower, eerie passages of reverbed metal scrapes through sudden dynamic shifts toward a violent close. Urashima's 2020 double-LP reissue, limited to 299 copies, reproduces the original gatefold artwork with a 12" insert.
