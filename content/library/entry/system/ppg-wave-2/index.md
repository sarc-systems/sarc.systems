Released in 1981, the PPG Wave 2 paired analog filters and amplifiers with digital oscillators capable of 1,920 selectable single-cycle waveforms — the fullest realization of Wolfgang Palm's wavetable synthesis, and an instrument that helped define the sound of early-1980s electronic pop.
