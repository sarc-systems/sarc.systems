Positioned on the [Pepsi Pavilion](/library/research/pepsi-pavilion/) floor, the Sound Modifier Console was the operator-facing control surface for the [Pepsi Pavilion Sound System](/library/research/pepsi-pavilion-sound-system/): from it, an operator could route any of the system's up to 32 live or recorded inputs through AM or FM modulation and filtering (or pass them through unmodified), then distribute the result across the dome's 37 speakers in real time, in patterns that could equally be pre-programmed. [Pauline Oliveros](/library/research/pauline-oliveros/) and Lowell Cross were among those who operated the console during the Pavilion's run, alongside [David Tudor](/library/research/david-tudor/) himself.
