Founded in Tokyo in 1962 by Tsutomu Kato and Tadashi Osanai as Keio Electronic Laboratories — Kato ran a nightclub, and Osanai, dissatisfied with its rhythm machine, convinced him to fund a better one — the company shipped its first product, the Donca-Matic DA-20, in 1963. It entered organs in 1967, taking the "Korg" name from "Keio" plus "organ," and synthesizers in 1973 with the Mini-Korg, going on to build the [MS-20](/library/research/korg-ms-20/), [Mono/Poly](/library/research/korg-monopoly/), [Polysix](/library/research/korg-polysix/), and [Wavestation](/library/research/korg-wavestation/).
