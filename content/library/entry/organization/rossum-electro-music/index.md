Founded by Dave Rossum in 2015 after a stint at voice-processing company Audience Inc. following E-mu's 2011 closure, Rossum Electro-Music makes sophisticated digital-processing modules in the Eurorack format — its first products were re-created and improved versions of circuits Rossum had originally developed for E-mu instruments.
