Gary Mundy established Broken Flag as his own imprint after [Ramleh](/library/research/ramleh/)'s first breakup in 1984, using it to keep the group's early cassette and vinyl recordings in circulation and to release the harsh, feedback-based power electronics that defined the UK scene of the period. The label has remained tied to Ramleh's own activity ever since, continuing to represent the group's catalog through its later noise-rock reunions.
