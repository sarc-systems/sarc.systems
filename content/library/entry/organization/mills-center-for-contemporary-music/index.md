Established in 1966 when the San Francisco Tape Music Center relocated to Mills College under a $15,000 grant, the Mills Center for Contemporary Music continued the earlier Center's work under founding director Pauline Oliveros, becoming a long-running hub for experimental composition on the West Coast.
