Founded in Osaka in 1972 by Ikutaro Kakehashi, formerly of Ace Tone, Roland built a broad synthesizer line from the semi-modular System 100 (1975) and the professional System 700 (1976) through the Jupiter-8 (1981) and the digital D-50 (1987).
