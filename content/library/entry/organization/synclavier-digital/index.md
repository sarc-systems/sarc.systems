Continuing the Synclavier name after New England Digital's 1993 closure, Synclavier Digital — with original co-creator Cameron Jones still involved — has produced the Arturia-collaboration Synclavier V plugin, the iOS Synclavier Go!, and the 2022 Synclavier Regen hardware synthesizer.
