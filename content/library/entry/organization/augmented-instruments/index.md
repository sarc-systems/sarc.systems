Formed in 2016 to continue research from Andrew McPherson and Victor Zappi's Augmented Instruments Laboratory at Queen Mary University of London, the company develops the Bela platform for ultra-low-latency embedded audio and sensor processing, following a successful Kickstarter campaign.
