Founded by Nurse With Wound's original members to release their own records, United Dairies put out the group's early landmark albums — *Merzbild Schwet* (1980), *To the Quiet Men from a Tiny Girl* (1980), *Insect and Individual Silenced* (1981), and, in an initial pressing of 5,000, *Homotopy to Marie* — before John Fothergill's departure left Steven Stapleton as sole curator, a role he has held ever since, extending the label's catalog to Current 93, The Lemon Kittens, and Volcano the Bear.
