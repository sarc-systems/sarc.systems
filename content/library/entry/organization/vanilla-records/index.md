Run by Michio Teshima from Maizuru, Kyoto, Vanilla Records was an early hub for the Japanese noise scene, releasing work by nearly all of its key acts between 1989 and 1995: [C.C.C.C.](/library/research/cccc/)'s *Reflexive Universe* (1991), [Aube](/library/research/aube/)'s *Hydrophobia* (1991) and *Flood-Gate* (1993), [Masonna](/library/research/masonna/)'s early *Shinsen na Clitoris* (1990), [Incapacitants](/library/research/incapacitants/)' *Extreme Gospel Nights* (1993–94), [Violent Onsen Geisha](/library/research/violent-onsen-geisha/)'s *Shocks! Shocks! Shocks!* (1991), [Monde Bruits](/library/research/monde-bruits/)'s *Portuguese Man-of-War* (1991), [Solmania](/library/research/solmania/)'s *Morphine Nocturne* (1992), and a 1990 [Merzbow](/library/research/merzbow/) live recording from Boston.
