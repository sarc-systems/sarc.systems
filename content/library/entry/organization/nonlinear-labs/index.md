Founded in Berlin in 2011 by Stephan Schmitt — previously founder and longtime CTO of Native Instruments — Nonlinear Labs builds expressive digital instruments manufactured largely in Germany and Europe, unveiling the C15 keyboard synthesizer in 2016.
