Gil Mellé's 1971 score for Robert Wise's *The Andromeda Strain*, built entirely on modular synthesizer to score a microbiology-lab thriller — the first all-synthesizer score for a feature film, following his all-electronic main title for television's *Night Gallery* the year before.
