Recorded over two weeks in February 1970 at Dartmouth's Bregman Electronic Music Studio and released the same year on Bob Thiele's Flying Dutchman label, *Human Music* set Don Cherry's trumpet and voice against Jon Appleton's electronic synthesis across four tracks — "BOA," "OBA," "ABO," and "BAO" — an early crossing of electronic composition, jazz, and Cherry's ethnological interests. It won a Swing Journal award in 1971.
