Composed for the single-take handcar journey carrying the Writer, the Professor, and the Stalker into the Zone in Andrei Tarkovsky's *Stalker* (1979), "Train" combines musicalized railroad noise with Eduard Artemyev's synthesizer drone, extending the sequence's near-infinite, hypnotic duration.
