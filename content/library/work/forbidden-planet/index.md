Louis and Bebe Barron's score for the 1956 film *Forbidden Planet*, built entirely from custom electronic circuits driven into feedback and decay rather than any conventional instrument — the first wholly electronic film soundtrack, credited on screen only as "electronic tonalities" for want of a musicians' union classification. The score went unreleased commercially until this 1976 LP.
