One of four seminal solo albums Alvin Curran recorded through the 1970s, later gathered onto New World Records' *Solo Works: The '70s* compilation, *The Works* extends the collective-improvisation practice Curran developed with Musica Elettronica Viva into his own solo electroacoustic composition.
