Pressed in only 300 copies on Alchemy Records in 1989, *Repo* is an early, uncompromisingly direct [Incapacitants](/library/incapacitants/) statement — piercing high frequencies and distorted texture on Side A, pulsating beats set against dissonance on Side B — released years before collage-based approaches became common in noise. [Urashima](/library/urashima/)'s 2023 reissue was remastered by Lasse Marhaug.
