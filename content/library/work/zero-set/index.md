Recorded at [Conny Plank](/library/conny-plank/)'s studio in September 1982 and released on Sky Records in 1983, *Zero Set* is the only album by the trio of [Dieter Moebius](/library/dieter-moebius/), Plank, and Guru Guru drummer Mani Neumeier — Moebius had come to know Neumeier as [Harmonia](/library/harmonia/)'s guest drummer on *[Deluxe](/library/deluxe-harmonia/)*, and here set his and Plank's electronic sequence patterns directly against Neumeier's hyperactive live percussion, making the record one of krautrock's earliest extensions toward the dance floor.
