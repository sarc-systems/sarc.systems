Titled after the opening word of James Joyce's *Finnegans Wake*, *Riverrun*
was built entirely from real-time granular synthesis on a DMX-1000 digital
signal processor, at densities up to 2,375 grains per second — the first
piece to explore the range between synchronic and asynchronic granular
textures, and a landmark in Barry Truax's development of the technique. It
won the Magisterium prize at the 1991 Bourges International Competition of
Electroacoustic Music.
