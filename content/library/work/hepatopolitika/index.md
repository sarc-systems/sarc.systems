Originally released in 1994 on Praxis Dr. Bearmann in a numbered edition of only 125 copies on transparent vinyl, *Hepatopolitika* is one of [K2](/library/k2/)'s most exhausting works — cut-up noise built from crashing, creaking, and scraping junk metal, edited in jarring, unpredictable bursts, and closing with Kimihide Kusafuka's own manifesto: "Noise cannot be refused by either ears and heads!" [Urashima](/library/urashima/) reissued it on vinyl in 2019.
