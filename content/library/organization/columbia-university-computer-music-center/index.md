Renamed in 1996 from the Columbia-Princeton Electronic Music Center under co-directors Fred Lerdahl and Brad Garton, reflecting computers' having superseded the earlier analog equipment, Columbia's Computer Music Center continues as the oldest continuously operating electroacoustic music research center in the United States.
