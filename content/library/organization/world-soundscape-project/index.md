Founded by R. Murray Schafer at Simon Fraser University in the late 1960s,
the World Soundscape Project (WSP) treated the acoustic environment itself
as an object of study and composition, producing surveys such as *The
Vancouver Soundscape* (1973) and the *Handbook for Acoustic Ecology* (1978).
Its core researchers — Schafer, Barry Truax, Hildegard Westerkamp, Howard
Broomfield, Peter Huse, and Bruce Davis — went on to found the field of
acoustic ecology.
