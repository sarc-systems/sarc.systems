Founded by Daphne Oram in 1959 after she left the BBC Radiophonic Workshop she had just co-founded, Oramics Studios for Electronic Composition was Britain's first independent electronic music studio — and Oram the first woman to run one. There she developed Oramics, a technique for drawing directly onto sprocketed film strips to generate and control sound.
