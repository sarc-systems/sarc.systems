Cosey Fanni Tutti (born Christine Carol Newby, 1951, Hull) — her stage name suggested by mail artist Robin Klassnick, after Mozart's *Così fan tutte* — was a founding member of COUM Transmissions in 1969, representing Britain as an installation artist at the 1975 Biennale de Paris, before co-founding [Throbbing Gristle](/library/throbbing-gristle/) in 1975 on guitar, cornet, tapes, and vocals. After the group's 1981 split she continued recording with [Chris Carter](/library/chris-carter/) as Chris & Cosey, renamed Carter Tutti around 2000. She has published two memoirs, *Art Sex Music* (2017) and *Re-Sisters* (2022).
