Rather than a fixed installation experienced passively, the [Pepsi Pavilion](/library/pepsi-pavilion/) hosted a changing program of live presentation for the run of [Expo '70](/library/expo-70/): resident and guest artists operated the [Sound Modifier Console](/library/pepsi-pavilion-sound-modifier-console/) and lighting controls in real time, drawing on David Tudor's library of program tapes to reprogram the Pavilion's sound and light for each session. [Pauline Oliveros](/library/pauline-oliveros/) and Lowell Cross were among the artists who operated the console, alongside [David Tudor](/library/david-tudor/) himself, while Ritty Burchfield served as the program's software librarian, keeping the tape library available to whichever guest programmer was on the floor.
