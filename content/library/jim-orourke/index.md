Jim O'Rourke (b. 1969, Chicago) emerged from that city's experimental and improvised music scene in the late 1980s, working across labels including Sound of Pig, Staalplaat, and Skin Graft before forming a long duo with David Grubbs as [Gastr del Sol](/library/gastr-del-sol/) (1993–1998). He joined [Sonic Youth](/library/sonic-youth/) as an official member from 1999 to 2005, and from 1997 was a member of the improvised computer-music trio Fenn O'Berg alongside [Fennesz](/library/fennesz/) and [Peter Rehberg](/library/peter-rehberg/). His solo catalog splits between song-oriented Drag City albums — *Bad Timing* (1997), *Eureka* (1999), *Insignificance* (2001) — and an ongoing Bandcamp archival series, Steamroom, built largely around a [Serge modular synthesizer](/library/serge-modular-synthesizer/). As a producer he mixed Wilco's *Yankee Hotel Foxtrot* and produced their Grammy-winning *A Ghost Is Born*, and produced [John Fahey](/library/john-fahey/)'s 1997 album *Womblife* during Fahey's late-career turn toward the avant-garde. Since 2017 he has restored and mastered [Roland Kayn](/library/roland-kayn/)'s cybernetic-music catalog for its ongoing posthumous release. [Tony Conrad](/library/tony-conrad/) contributed to Gastr del Sol's *Upgrade & Afterlife* (1996). O'Rourke relocated to Japan around 2000, where he continues to score films and record with Eiko Ishibashi.
