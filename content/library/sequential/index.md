Founded by Dave Smith in San Jose in 1974 as Sequential Circuits, the company released the Prophet-5 in 1978 — the first fully programmable polyphonic synthesizer, and the first musical instrument built around an embedded microprocessor — before Smith led the development of the MIDI specification in 1981, coining the term itself. Sequential Circuits closed and its name passed to Yamaha in 1987; Smith continued building instruments as Dave Smith Instruments from 2002, and in 2018, after Yamaha returned the name in 2015, rebranded the company back to Sequential.
