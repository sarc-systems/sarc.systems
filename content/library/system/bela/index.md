Developed from 2014 by Andrew McPherson and Victor Zappi at Queen Mary University of London (initially as "BeagleRT"), Bela pairs a custom audio/sensor cape with the BeagleBone Black single-board computer for embedded instruments, sound installations, and effects with roughly 1ms latency.
