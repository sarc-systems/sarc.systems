Developed by Serge Tcherepnin with CalArts students Rich Gold, Randy Cohen, and Rex Probe from 1972 and sold by Serge Modular Music Systems, the Serge system favored patchable, general-purpose function generators over the fixed-role oscillator/filter/envelope modules of contemporary designs, aiming for a flexible instrument affordable to students and hobbyists. Its signature Wave Multiplier modules generate timbral complexity through [wavefolding](/library/wavefolding/) rather than subtractive filtering, a defining trait of the system's sound.
