Ennio Morricone (1928–2020) was an Italian composer world-renowned for his film scores — including for Sergio Leone's Westerns — who was also, from its 1964 founding, a member of Franco Evangelisti's Gruppo di Improvvisazione Nuova Consonanza, applying the same collective, noise-embracing improvisation to concert work alongside his commercial career.
