Released on Lovely Music in 1987, *Leapday Night* gathers three pieces — the title work, "A Traveler's Dream Journal," and "Interspecies Smalltalk" — pairing Behrman's own live electronics with Rhys Chatham and Ben Neill on trumpet and mutantrumpet and Fluxus mainstay Takehisa Kosugi on violin.
