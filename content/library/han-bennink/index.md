Han Bennink (b. 1942) co-founded the Instant Composers Pool with Misha Mengelberg and Willem Breuker in 1967, becoming a central figure in Dutch improvised music. He also crossed into the British free-improvisation scene, performing on *The Topography of the Lungs* (1970) with Derek Bailey and Evan Parker — the first release on their newly founded Incus Records.
