The Studio for Advanced Research in Cybernetics builds and documents
experimental systems at the boundary of computation, control, and sound. This
site is its working record — a **Journal** of dated entries tracking current
work, beginning with the [SARC‑100](/journal/) and its associated videos.

Git and Markdown are the archive. Entries are historical: written once, dated,
and left standing.
