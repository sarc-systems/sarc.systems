**SARC** is an independent studio for the design, construction, and documentation of experimental systems
— instruments, control structures, and computational processes made audible. Documentation is part of the
work, not a record kept alongside it.

## This site

- **[Journal]({{< relref "journal" >}})** — dated records of SARC activity: worklogs, experiments, field
  notes, videos, and essays tied to a particular moment. Once published, an entry is not rewritten.
- **[Library]({{< relref "library" >}})** — curated references and precedents that inform SARC's work.
- **Systems** — living documentation of the systems built at SARC, updated as those systems change. Not
  yet public.

## Library

The Library holds material with a concrete relationship to SARC's research — precedents, references,
instruments, documents, people, and organizations connected to specific systems and lines of work. It is
curated, not comprehensive: not a discography, an encyclopedia, or a catalog of things the studio happens
to find interesting.

The Library is organized into Collections, each a bounded body of material around a particular domain. An
entry can belong to more than one Collection when it genuinely spans domains — and those overlaps are
often where the Library is most useful.
